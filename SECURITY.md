# Security

TastyTunes runs on your own computer and talks to your streamer and local media servers over
your local network. If you find a way it could be misused, please report it privately before
sharing it anywhere else.

## How to report

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository (the Security tab, then
"Report a vulnerability"), or the TastyTunes [contact form](https://tastytunes.app/help/).
Please include the version of TastyTunes, your platform, and the steps that show the problem.

## What to expect

A reply within a week, a fix in the next release when the report is confirmed, and credit in
the release notes if you would like it.

## In scope

- The app itself, on macOS, Windows and Linux
- The optional MCP server for AI agents, and its token
- Action links (tastytunes://)
- The keys and tokens TastyTunes stores
- The requests it sends to the internet services listed under
  [What leaves your machine](README.md#what-leaves-your-machine)
